A SMALL QUESTION. A GOOD PLACE TO START.

What deserves
a little more oh?

We follow our curiosity into real-fruit crunch, coffee by the cube, a fruit tea café in your bag, and candy with a surprisingly cool finish. Different ideas, each made around a specific taste, texture or everyday ritual.

Vitablabla started in a Yale kitchen during late-night study sessions. Now based in Bethesda, Maryland, we still believe that familiar things can become a good little discovery.
